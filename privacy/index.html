Privacy Policy

C’Freddo Kitchen Calculator

Publisher: C’Freddo
Application: C’Freddo Kitchen Calculator
Applicable version: 1.1 for Android
Intended audience: All ages, including children
Last updated: November 18, 2026

C’Freddo respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains the data used by C’Freddo Kitchen Calculator, how it is stored, and how Android system features may affect data backups.

This policy applies to version 1.1 of the Android application, based on its technical operation as reviewed before publication.

1. Data collection and sharing

C’Freddo Kitchen Calculator operates locally on your device.

The reviewed version contains no advertising, user accounts, analytics tools, or user-tracking features. It does not include an app-initiated network transmission mechanism and does not request Internet access.

C’Freddo does not transmit your calculations, preferences, or timers to a server operated by C’Freddo.

Android system backups and diagnostic services operate separately from the application's networking behavior. They are described later in this policy.

2. Data stored locally

To function properly, the application uses certain data stored in its private Android application storage.

  • General preferences: appearance, theme, language, and sound preferences.
  • Custom presets: names, categories, durations, alerts, and messages created by the user.
  • Timers: names, durations, deadlines, and state information required to operate and restore timers.
  • Alerts: technical information used to manage notifications, alert delivery, and the prevention of duplicate alerts.
  • Custom ringtones: the URI reference and name of the selected sound. C’Freddo does not copy the audio file itself.

3. Calculator history

The calculator may temporarily keep up to 20 calculations in the application's memory.

This history is not stored as persistent application data and is not included in C’Freddo's Android backups.

You can clear the history from the calculator. It is also lost when the application process ends.

4. Notifications and alarms

C’Freddo uses Android notification and alarm features to operate its kitchen timers.

On Android 13 and later, the application may request permission to display notifications.

It may also request special access to exact alarms so that certain deadlines can be scheduled more precisely.

If the necessary permissions are not granted, some notifications may not appear and approximate alarms may be delayed by Android.

Notifications may display the timer name and alert message. Whether this information appears on the lock screen depends on your device's notification privacy settings.

5. Custom ringtones

The application may use Android's ringtone picker to let you select a custom sound.

C’Freddo stores a reference to the selected sound and its name, but does not copy the audio file into its application data.

If the selected ringtone becomes unavailable, including after a device transfer, the application may use its built-in sound instead.

6. Android backups and transfers

Android may back up certain private application data through its backup service or during a transfer between devices.

Depending on your device settings and the services available, the following data may be backed up:

  • General preferences and appearance.
  • Language preferences.
  • Sound preferences.
  • Custom presets.

C’Freddo's backup rules exclude persistent timer data and alert delivery state from configured Android cloud backups and transfers between Android devices.

Timers remain stored normally on their original device, allowing them to resume after the application closes or the device restarts.

A backup created before these rules were introduced may still contain older timer data.

Android services or device manufacturers control the availability, retention, and restoration of their backups. C’Freddo cannot directly delete copies retained by those services.

The security and encryption mechanisms protecting these backups are provided by the relevant system backup service.

7. Android diagnostic logs

The application may write technical information to Android diagnostic logs to help investigate certain timer behaviors.

In the reviewed version, some diagnostic logs may also contain the text of a custom alert entered by the user.

C’Freddo does not directly transmit these logs to its own servers. The logs remain subject to Android system mechanisms and may appear in a diagnostic report shared by the user or processed by the system.

8. Data retention

Preferences are retained until they are changed or the application's local data is cleared.

Custom presets are retained until the user deletes them or clears the application's data.

Timer and alert states are stored locally to support their operation and restoration. Some technical alert delivery records have no automatic deletion period defined in the reviewed code.

9. Data deletion

You can clear C’Freddo's local application data through the Android settings, in the application's storage section.

You can also uninstall the application. This normally removes its local data from the device.

However, an earlier Android backup may remain stored by the backup service and may later be restored.

The application does not include user accounts or an accessible feature to export or globally erase all data, including any system backups.

10. Data protection

C’Freddo's persistent data is mainly stored in the application's private storage, which is protected by Android system security mechanisms.

The protection of backups, notifications, and system logs also depends on your device's mechanisms and settings.

11. Children's privacy

C’Freddo Kitchen Calculator is intended for users of all ages, including children.

The reviewed version does not require user accounts and contains no advertising or user-tracking system.

The application does not directly request personal information from children. Information voluntarily entered in the calculation and timer tools is processed locally, subject to the Android mechanisms described in this policy.

12. Changes to this policy

C’Freddo may update this Privacy Policy if the application's operation or applicable requirements change.

Any revised version will be published on this page with a corresponding update date.
